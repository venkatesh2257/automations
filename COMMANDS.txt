           BIBLE SHORTS & REELS AI AUTOMATION - QUICK COMMAND GUIDE
================================================================================
Series Structure: Genesis to Revelation (1 Chapter -> 3 Videos Per Day)
Accounts Linked : YouTube Channel & Instagram (@biblewithai7)

[1] DAILY AUTOMATION COMMAND (RUN TODAY'S CHAPTER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To generate all 3 videos for today's Bible chapter and automatically upload
them to BOTH YouTube Shorts (scheduled for 9 AM, 2 PM, 7 PM) and Instagram Reels:

    start-youtube

* Note: After finishing, it automatically advances the chapter pointer for tomorrow!


[2] CHECK YOUR BIBLE CHAPTER PROGRESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To see which book and chapter is scheduled next and how many videos are completed:

    start-youtube --status


[3] JUMP / RESET TO ANY BOOK AND CHAPTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you want to start from or jump to a specific book and chapter:

    start-youtube --set-position Genesis 1
    start-youtube --set-position Exodus 14
    start-youtube --set-position Matthew 1
    start-youtube --set-position Revelation 1


[4] RUN A SPECIFIC CHAPTER ON-DEMAND (WITHOUT CHANGING SAVED PROGRESS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To make 3 videos for a specific chapter without altering your daily schedule:

    start-youtube --book Genesis --chapter 1
    start-youtube --book Daniel --chapter 6


[5] TEST GENERATE LOCALLY (NO UPLOADS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To render the 3 videos locally onto your computer without posting to social media:

    start-youtube --no-upload


[6] 100% HANDS-FREE AUTOMATIC DAILY SCHEDULING (CRON JOB)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To make your computer automatically generate and schedule 3 videos every night 
at midnight (00:00) while you sleep:

1. In your terminal, type:
    crontab -e

2. Paste this line at the bottom and save:
    0 0 * * * /home/user/.local/bin/start-youtube >> /home/user/Desktop/automation/bible_shorts_ai/output/cron.log 2>&1
